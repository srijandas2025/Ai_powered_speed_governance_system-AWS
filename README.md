\# AI-Powered Smart Speed Governance System (AWS)



An AI-driven smart traffic governance solution designed specifically for Indian roads.  

This system leverages Artificial Intelligence and AWS cloud services to monitor vehicle speed, detect violations, and support ethical, scalable, and data-driven road safety enforcement.



---



\## 🚦 Problem Statement



Over-speeding is one of the leading causes of road accidents in India.  

Traditional speed monitoring systems are:

\- Reactive rather than proactive

\- Limited in scalability

\- Lacking intelligent analysis and automation



There is a need for a \*\*smart, AI-powered, and cloud-scalable speed governance system\*\* that can support Indian traffic conditions while maintaining ethical and responsible AI practices.



---



\## 💡 Solution Overview



The \*\*AI-Powered Smart Speed Governance System\*\* uses:

\- AI-based vehicle detection and speed estimation

\- Automated rule-based decision making

\- AWS cloud infrastructure for scalability and reliability



The system is designed to assist traffic authorities by providing \*\*real-time insights\*\*, \*\*violation detection\*\*, and \*\*data-backed governance\*\* rather than replacing human decision-making.



---



\## 🧠 Key Features



\- AI-based vehicle detection and speed estimation  

\- Real-time speed violation identification  

\- Cloud-based data storage and processing using AWS  

\- Scalable architecture suitable for Indian road networks  

\- Ethical AI approach with transparency and accountability  

\- Designed for governance support, not surveillance misuse  



---



\## 🏗️ System Architecture (High Level)



1\. Road-side camera captures vehicle footage  

2\. AI model processes video frames for vehicle detection  

3\. Speed is estimated using frame-based motion analysis  

4\. Violation data is securely sent to AWS cloud  

5\. Authorities receive insights via dashboards or alerts  



\*(Detailed design available in `design.md`)\*



---



\## ☁️ AWS Services Used



\- Amazon EC2 – AI model deployment  

\- Amazon S3 – Secure data storage  

\- Amazon Lambda – Event-driven processing  

\- Amazon DynamoDB – Metadata \& logs  

\- Amazon CloudWatch – Monitoring and logging  



---



\## 🔐 Responsible AI \& Ethics



This project strictly follows \*\*Responsible AI principles\*\*:

\- No facial recognition is used  

\- Focuses only on vehicles, not individuals  

\- Data minimization and privacy awareness  

\- Human-in-the-loop decision support  

\- Transparent and explainable AI logic  



More details are documented in `responsible-ai.md`.
