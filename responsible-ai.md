\# Responsible AI Statement



\## Ethical Use of AI

This system uses AI only for vehicle speed analysis and does not identify individuals.



\## Privacy Protection

\- No facial recognition is performed

\- No personal identity data is stored

\- All data is securely stored using AWS services



\## Bias Reduction

The system focuses on vehicle behavior, not driver demographics, ensuring fairness.



\## Human Oversight

AI-generated results are reviewed by traffic authorities before any action is taken.



\## Compliance

Designed in alignment with Indian traffic regulations and ethical AI practices.
